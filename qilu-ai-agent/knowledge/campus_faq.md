# Qilu Campus Service FAQ

## Campus card lost

If a campus card is lost, report the loss at the campus card service center as soon as possible. Bring student ID or other valid identity proof for replacement.

## Dormitory repair

For dormitory water, electricity, door, window, furniture, or network problems, create a repair ticket. Include building, room number, issue description, photos if available, and an available visit time.

## Printing service

Library printing points support document printing, copying, and binding. If campus card payment or printer recognition fails, contact the printing point staff and keep the failed payment record.

## Express pickup

Campus express station handles parcel pickup, pickup code exceptions, and missing parcel follow-up. Bring pickup code and valid identity proof.

## Career consultation

Career consultation office supports resume review, interview preparation, employment policy questions, and career planning appointments.
